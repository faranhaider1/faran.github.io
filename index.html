CHAPTER 01

Where It Started

I was born and raised in Lahore, Pakistan. From an early age, I was fascinated by technology, problem-solving, and the idea of building things that create value. What began as curiosity eventually evolved into a passion for innovation, entrepreneurship, and continuous learning.

CHAPTER 02

Turkey

During my bachelor's studies, I had the opportunity to participate in an exchange program in Izmir, Turkey. Living abroad for the first time broadened my perspective, introduced me to new cultures, and strengthened my ability to adapt to unfamiliar environments. It was one of the experiences that encouraged me to think beyond borders.

CHAPTER 03

Germany

Germany became one of the most transformative chapters of my life. I moved here to pursue my Master's degree in Computer Science and have spent several years growing academically, professionally, and personally. The experience taught me independence, discipline, and the importance of long-term thinking. Germany is where I discovered a deeper appreciation for quality, structure, and lifelong learning.
